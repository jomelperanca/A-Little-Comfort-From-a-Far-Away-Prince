A Letter From Your Loveyy 💌

Open this again whenever the day feels like too much.

Hey, my Sweet Babyy,

Hi, love. ♡ If you're reading this after waking up from a long, tiring day, I really wish I could be right there with you. I'd hold your hand, listen to you talk about everything that happened, or sit quietly beside you if words felt like too much.

I know I can't magically make all the stress disappear from far away. I wish I could, though. So I made you this little gift instead, a virtual place where you don't have to be productive, cheerful, or strong for anybody. You can just be yourself here.

Please remember that one difficult day doesn't erase all the effort you've put in. You are allowed to feel tired. You're allowed to take a break. You don't have to apologize for having a hard time, and you don't have to make your feelings smaller to make other people comfortable.

If I were there, I'd probably give you a very long hug and make some delicious pasta or just drown you with kisses. Since we're apart right now, imagine that hug lasting as long as you need. I'm proud of you for getting through today, even if all you managed was to make it to this moment.

Whenever you feel like it, you can tell me about your day. I will listen to you all day long. Let me have a share of your burden and keep each other company. You don't have to feel better immediately. I'll still care about you while you're figuring it out.

Drink a little water if you can, relax your shoulders, and be gentle with yourself tonight. You matter to me on your happiest days and your hardest ones, too.

Sending you all my love, from my little corner of the universe to yours. ♡
