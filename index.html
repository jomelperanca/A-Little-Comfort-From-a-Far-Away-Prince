To my lovely boy,

You don't have to pretend you're okay with me. You can be tired, quiet, frustrated, or sad. You don't have to explain everything perfectly, either. I love you on your bright days, and I love you on the days when all you can do is get through the next hour.

If I could borrow a little magic from this fantasy world, I'd use it to give you a soft place to land. Since I can't do that through a screen, let this tiny website be a reminder: somewhere in this big world, there's a heart that is cheering for you. Mine. Always.

Drink some water, take a breath, and be gentle with yourself, okay? One little step at a time. When you're ready, tell me about your day—or we can talk about absolutely nothing together.
